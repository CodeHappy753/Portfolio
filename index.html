About

Tech has been my thing for most of my life. I started out building my own computers, and for the last few years I've been building software people rely on every day: a real-time telemetry portal that monitors power generation equipment in the field, internal apps for files and forms, and data pipelines and reports that turn raw data into decisions. Before software was my job, I worked in field operations and parts inventory, so I build tools with the people using them in mind.

I'm big on learning how to learn. I use AI tools like Claude and GitHub Copilot to build my own study plans, break down new technology and speed up my work. I like puzzles that keep my mind sharp, which may explain why I enjoy debugging.

Off the clock I play piano and guitar, work out, and play games with my wife. My desk setup gets the same question on every video call: "Do you run a podcast? Do you stream?" Yes and no. I stream once in a while, and the podcast is still waiting for its first guest.
